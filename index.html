ABOUT VANDANA VILLA

Space to Slow Down, Moments to Remember

Vandana Villa is a thoughtfully designed private getaway in Gorai, Mumbai, created for families and groups who value comfort, privacy and quality time together.

With spacious living areas, comfortable bedrooms, kitchens and a private pool, the villa brings together the ease of a home with the feeling of a relaxed holiday escape.

Whether it is a family gathering, a quiet break or an unhurried weekend away, Vandana Villa offers a comfortable setting to spend time together.
