How to Reach

Getting to Vandana's Villa is easy from different parts of Mumbai.

By Train

Reach Borivali Railway Station, then take an auto or bus to Gorai Creek and a ferry to Gorai.

By Ferry

Take the Gorai Ferry from Gorai Creek. The villa is just a few minutes from the jetty.

By Road

Drive directly to Vandana's Villa using Google Maps for the easiest route.
