HOW TO REACH

Getting to Vandana Villa

Choose the route that works best for your journey.

By Railway

Reach Borivali Railway Station and continue your journey towards Vandana Villa by road.

Full Guide
  1. Reach Borivali Railway Station.
  2. From the station, start your road journey towards Vandana Villa, Gorai.
  3. Follow the complete Google Maps route.
  4. The route will guide you directly towards the villa.
Full Railway Route Guide on Google Maps

By Ferry

Take the Gorai–Borivali ferry route for a convenient journey towards the Gorai side and continue towards Vandana Villa.

Full Guide
  1. Reach Borivali Railway Station.
  2. From the station, travel to the Gorai–Borivali Ferry Point.
  3. Take the Gorai–Borivali ferry.
  4. After reaching the other side by boat, take the Ambedkar Nagar ticket.
  5. Continue your journey towards Vandana Villa.
  6. Use the complete Google Maps route for the remaining journey.

THINGS TO BE REMEMBERED

House Rules

A few simple rules help keep your stay comfortable, peaceful and enjoyable for everyone.

CHECK-IN 1:00 PM
CHECK-OUT 11:00 AM
🕐

Quiet Hours & Pool Timing

Please keep music and noise low during late hours so everyone can enjoy a peaceful stay. Pool timing should be followed as communicated by the villa team.

🏊

In the Pool

No diving, jumping or unsafe stunts. Please do not eat or smoke in the pool. Keep sharp objects away from the pool area.

🏡

A Family-First Villa

Vandana Villa is designed for families, friends and private group stays. Loud parties and events are not permitted.

🐾

Pets

Pets are not allowed at the property.

🚭

Smoking & Alcohol

Smoking and alcohol should only be used in permitted areas. Smoking is not allowed in the pool or bedrooms. Please dispose of waste responsibly.

🚗

Parking

Parking is available for guests at the property. Please follow the caretaker's instructions while parking vehicles.

👥

Guest Capacity & Extra Guests

10 guests are included. Up to 5 extra guests are allowed at ₹1,000 per extra guest. Maximum capacity is 15 guests.

🛠️

Property Damage

Any damage to the property, furniture, appliances, fixtures or other items caused by guests will be chargeable according to the actual repair or replacement value.

🧹

Care & Cleanliness

Please treat the villa like your own home. Keep the property clean and avoid moving, misusing or damaging furniture, appliances and other villa items.

🛡️

Security & Privacy

Please respect the privacy and security of the property. Common-area security measures may be used to maintain a safe environment.

⚡

Power & Backup

Inverter power backup is available for essential needs during power interruptions. Please use electricity thoughtfully during an outage.
