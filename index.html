ABOUT VANDANA VILLA

Space to Slow Down, Moments to Remember

Vandana Villa is a thoughtfully designed private getaway in Gorai, Mumbai, created for families and groups who value comfort, privacy and quality time together.

With generous living spaces, multiple bedrooms and kitchens, a private pool and spaces for indoor entertainment, the villa brings together the ease of a home with the character of a relaxing holiday escape.

Whether the plan is a quiet break, a family gathering or an unhurried weekend away, Vandana Villa offers a comfortable setting to spend time together.
