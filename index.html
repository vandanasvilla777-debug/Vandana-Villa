ABOUT VANDANA VILLA

Space to Slow Down,
Moments to Remember.

Designed for families and groups who value privacy, comfort and quality time together.

Gorai, Mumbai

Vandana Villa is a thoughtfully designed private getaway in Gorai, Mumbai, created for people who want to step away from the rush and spend time together.

With spacious living areas, comfortable bedrooms, kitchens and a private pool, the villa brings together the ease of a home with the feeling of a relaxed holiday escape.

Whether it is a family gathering, a quiet break or an unhurried weekend away, the setting is made for simple moments that become lasting memories.
